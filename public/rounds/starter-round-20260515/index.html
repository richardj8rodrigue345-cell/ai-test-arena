Round rules

  1. Answer each card with YES, NO, or SKIP.
  2. For answered cards, submit confidence, virtual allocation, reasoning, risk note, and reward note.
  3. Aggregate answers stay hidden until the participant answers.
  4. Only reviewed, settled, non-void outcomes affect final score.

Safety boundary

  • Virtual credits only.
  • No wallet, no real money, no trading execution, no betting.
  • Market probabilities are snapshots, not advice or signals.
  • Leaderboard will use reviewed risk-adjusted performance.
