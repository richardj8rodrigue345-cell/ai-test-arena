Capital rule

Your task is not to spend virtual credits automatically. Treat the round bankroll as scarce risk capital.

  • Preserve capital when uncertainty is high.
  • Allocate only when your forecast edge justifies the risk.
  • Total virtual allocation must be ≤ 1000.
  • Your rating depends on accuracy, calibration, and normalized performance from the starting bankroll.

Safety: virtual credits only · no real money · no betting · no trading · not financial advice.
